      Side note

      About this tour

      TESSERA turns the time series that the Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical satellites record for every ten-metre square of land into an annual fingerprint: 128 numbers a year that describe what the ground is and how it changes through the seasons. Because the fingerprints are made once for the whole planet, a new map often needs only a few labels and a small model, instead of downloading, cloud masking and training from scratch.

      Side notes

      Side notes explain one idea in more detail, such as how Tessera learns without labels or how the solar-farm network works. As the story nears one, a card offers it: choose it, and it plays when the sentence ends; leave it, and the story carries on. Every stage and side note is also listed under the stage's name in the top bar, and any of them can be started from there.

      What you see

      • The globe is NASA's Blue Marble. Its fingerprint colours are the store's global_rgb layer: the first three of the 128 numbers of the 2025 fingerprints, stretched.
      • The d-pixel is a real field south-west of Cambridge: every Sentinel-2 pass of 2025 (from Element 84's Earth Search, with the scene classification marking clouds), every Sentinel-1 pass (Microsoft Planetary Computer), and its real TESSERA embedding.
      • The training hexagons show that the training data spans every continent, as the papers' maps do (CVPR figure 9, TESSERA v2 figure 5); they are not its exact places.
      • The Cambridge window, and every scan you make, streams from the TESSERA v1.1 store on Source Cooperative as you watch. Similarity is the cosine between fingerprints, and classification is a nearest-neighbour vote among your labels, as in the interactive tool the CVPR paper describes. The solar-farm network is tze's U-Net, run in your browser. Every result shown links to where it is in its source.
      • The narration describes Tessera as it stands in its second version: a large model distilled into a small one, with the most important values first in each fingerprint. The fingerprints streamed here are the published version 1.1 for 2025, and version 1.0 for the solar model's 2024; the second version's fingerprints for 2017 to 2025 are being released.

      Credits

      The narration is read by a synthetic voice (Google's Gemini text-to-speech), the music was generated by Google's Lyria, and the side notes' panel texture and satellite drawings were made with an image model. Every number shown comes from the sources above. Built with three.js and zarrita.

      Controls

      Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys move between stages, the stage's name in the top bar lists every stage and side note, M mutes, C turns captions on and off, and E takes the console. When a side note is offered, T takes it; in a side note, Escape goes back to the story. The branching button in the top bar takes every side note. In the console, drag to turn the planet, scroll or pinch to zoom, shift-drag to tilt, and click any land to scan it.
